Why buyers look for further bidders

According to the European Court of Auditors, 42 % of all public contracts in the EU in 2021 were awarded although only one company had made an offer. This does not mean that every bidder wins. It does show that many procedures lack a second, suitable offer.

Source: European Court of Auditors, Special Report 28/2023
