Orders in markets where you have nobody yet

I am most useful to manufacturers with no office and no dealer in the buyer's country. If you already have a partner there, they should bid – and I will tell you so.

What I take on

  1. CheckRead the whole file: deadlines, guarantees, penalties, payment terms, local duties.
  2. PresentA short summary without the buyer's name – you decide whether it fits.
  3. AgreeA short written confirmation for this one deal. Then I name the buyer and the source.
  4. SupportForms, questions to the buyer, platform steps, coordinating translations.
  5. CloseYou deliver and invoice. Commission only after the buyer has paid you.

What I am not

  • Not a distributor: I do not buy and sell on my own account.
  • Not a local service partner: installation, maintenance and warranty stay with you.
  • Not an inspection body: I check documents and public information, not factories.
  • No promise that you will win.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost?

Nothing upfront. The commission rate is agreed in writing before you bid. No order, no commission.

We already knew this tender.

Then you object within five days with proof, and no commission is due.

Do you work exclusively?

Usually not. Every deal and every customer is registered in writing.

Who submits the offer?

You do. I make no declarations in your name.

Tell me what it is about

Manufacturers name product and target country, buyers their need.
