Frequently asked questions

What does your work cost me?

Nothing upfront. You only pay a commission on closed deals. We agree the rate in writing before we start.

Do you work for us exclusively?

Usually not exclusively. Every customer I bring is registered with you in writing – so it is always clear what a commission applies to.

What should I send you?

Catalogue or website, price list, minimum quantities, lead times and certificates. I reply with a first view of your target markets.
