Who we are

Why RS Handelsvertretung exists

Behind every request are people. This is our story – and where we are going.

How it all began

  1. Two worlds

    I have lived in India, Germany and Switzerland, and came to know two worlds that meet far too rarely: manufacturers who build with skill and pride – and buyers who are looking for good solutions.

  2. The first plan

    Early on, I wanted to start my own company together with a partner from the USA. It was too early. But the wish to bring people and companies together across borders stayed with me.

  3. Learning the trade

    In online trade I learned the craft: finding products, comparing suppliers, selling to customers. So I know both the buying and the selling side.

  4. The number that changed everything

    When I began to read purchase requests and tenders across Europe systematically, one number stood out: more than 40 % of items received just a single offer. Suitable manufacturers existed – they simply did not know, or forms in a foreign language put them off.

  5. Founding the company

    That is why I founded RS Handelsvertretung in Menziken, Switzerland: independent, commission only, with clear written agreements.

  6. The first partners

    Manufacturers from Europe and Asia have placed their trust in me. Together we have submitted the first tender offers – with translation, forms and questions to the buyer.

  7. Today

    Every day I read new requests, check conditions and costs, and look for the manufacturer that truly fits. Behind every request are people: a buyer who needs a solution, and an entrepreneur who hopes their work will be seen. For both, I want to be the one who answers, says honestly what fits – and stays with it.

Our vision

  1. 01

    Every request deserves more than one offer.

    In a large European procurement database, more than 40 % of items received just a single offer. More suitable offers mean better prices and better quality for the buyer – and new customers for good manufacturers.

  2. 02

    Opening Europe – for small makers too.

    Forms, platforms and foreign languages should never be the reason not to bid. I take care of the paperwork – the manufacturer focuses on the product.

  3. 03

    We get paid for success only.

    No retainer, no upfront costs. I earn only when my client wins an order – so we share the same goal.

  4. 04

    All costs on the table.

    Before anyone invests time, deadlines, guarantees, penalties and payment terms are laid out openly. No surprises.

Rajinder Singh

Independent commercial agent in Menziken, Switzerland. Personal network in India, Germany and Switzerland, plus contacts in Italy, the USA and the UK.
