Tenders

Public and private tenders that fit your products

Every day, buyers in Europe and worldwide put machines, equipment, furniture, medical and laboratory devices out to tender – contracts that foreign manufacturers could win too. I find the right ones and check the conditions before you invest time.

more than 40 % of items in a large European procurement database received just a single offer. Many orders are waiting for a second, better bidder.

  • Deadlines, place of delivery, guarantees and every cost in the contract – at a glance, before you invest time
  • Help with documents, translation, questions to the buyer and online submission
  • Commission only on award

Frequently asked questions

Which tenders are suitable?

Tenders for goods – machines, equipment, devices, materials – that are open to foreign manufacturers and leave enough time before the deadline.

What about bid bonds?

I check in advance whether a bid bond (bank guarantee or deposit) is required, and list all contract costs in the very first message.

Who submits the offer?

The manufacturer. I help with forms, translation, questions to the buyer and the online submission.
