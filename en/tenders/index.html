All conditions first. Then the decision.

Most tenders that look open carry a local duty: stock, language, service, a national mark. I look for those clauses before I write to you.

Why buyers look for further bidders

According to the European Court of Auditors, 42 % of all public contracts in the EU in 2021 were awarded although only one company had made an offer. This does not mean that every bidder wins. It does show that many procedures lack a second, suitable offer.

Source: European Court of Auditors, Special Report 28/2023

What I check before every message

  • Bid bond or deposit – I do not propose tenders that require one
  • Enough time before the deadline
  • Local duties: stock, on-site service, contract language, national marks
  • Award criteria, penalties, guarantees, payment schedule
  • Whether the model asked for really is in your range
