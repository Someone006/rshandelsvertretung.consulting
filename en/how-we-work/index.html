How we work

Clearly agreed before the work begins

Every cooperation starts with a short written agreement, so that both sides know from day one what applies.

  1. 1

    Request

    Manufacturers send catalogue and price list, buyers describe what they need.

  2. 2

    Check & agreement

    I check market and fit. Names and details follow a short written agreement.

  3. 3

    Contact & offer

    I make the introduction and support samples, quotation and negotiation.

  4. 4

    Deal

    Commission is due only when a deal is closed – nothing else.

Frequently asked questions

When do I learn the buyer's name?

After a short written agreement for the specific deal. Before that, I describe the need, the region and the conditions.

Who delivers and who is liable?

Delivery, warranty and guarantees stay with the manufacturer. I arrange and support the deal.

How fast do you answer?

Personally, and usually the same day.
