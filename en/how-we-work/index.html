What you sign – in plain words

For a single deal a short confirmation of a few pages is enough. This is what it says:

The one-deal confirmation

  • It covers only the one deal described – no agency for a market.
  • The commission rate is fixed in writing before you bid.
  • Commission only if you sell – and only once the buyer pays you.
  • I name the buyer after signature. If you can show you already had the request, you object within five days.
  • You alone are responsible for product, delivery, service and warranty.
  • Either side can end it with 30 days' notice; the registered deal stays protected.
  • Swiss law.

Integrity

  • Only the seller pays me. I accept nothing from buyers without disclosing it to you in writing first.
  • No commission and no advantage for a buyer's staff – ever.
  • I make no declarations in your name and sign nothing for you.
  • Before signature I use only publicly available information.
