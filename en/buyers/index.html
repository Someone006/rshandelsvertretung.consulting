One contact in Switzerland, delivery direct from the manufacturer

I represent selected manufacturers to Swiss business customers. You receive a quotation, data sheets and terms delivered to Switzerland – the contract is made directly with the manufacturer.

Manufacturers I represent

I tell you on request which manufacturers I represent for your product. On this website I name a manufacturer only with its written consent.

How it runs

  • You name product, quantity, dimensions and place of delivery
  • You receive the manufacturer's written quotation with a price delivered to Switzerland
  • Samples on request
  • Order, delivery, invoice and warranty directly with the manufacturer

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost me anything?

No. The manufacturer pays the commission.

Who is liable for the goods?

The manufacturer, as your contract partner.

Is my request confidential?

Yes. I pass your name only to the manufacturer you ask.

Tell me what it is about

Manufacturers name product and target country, buyers their need.
