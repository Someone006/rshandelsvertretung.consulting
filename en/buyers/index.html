For buyers

Reliable suppliers worldwide – checked before you ask

Looking for a second source, better value or a manufacturer for a custom part? I look for suitable manufacturers in Europe, Asia and other regions and check them in advance.

  • Manufacturers instead of traders – with references and certificates (e.g. ISO 9001, ISO 13485, IATF 16949)
  • Comparison of price, lead time and minimum quantities
  • A second, checked source, so that no purchase depends on a single supplier
  • Free of charge for you as a buyer – the supplier pays the commission

Frequently asked questions

Does this cost me anything?

No. The supplier pays the commission, and only when a deal is done.

How do you check a manufacturer?

Before every recommendation I check: do they make the product themselves? Do model and certificates fit? Are there references? Who is the right contact?

Is my request confidential?

Yes. I pass on your name only with your consent.
