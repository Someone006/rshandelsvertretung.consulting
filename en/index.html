For buyers

Reliable suppliers worldwide – checked before you ask

Looking for a second source, better value or a maker for a custom part? I search for suitable manufacturers in Europe, Asia and other regions and check them first.

  • Manufacturers, not middlemen – with references and certificates (e.g. ISO 9001, ISO 13485, IATF 16949)
  • Comparison of price, lead time and minimum order quantities
  • Free for you as a buyer – the supplier pays the commission

Tenders

  • Help with documents, questions to the buyer and your offer
  • Commission only if you win
