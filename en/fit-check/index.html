Does a tender abroad fit your company?

Six questions, one honest assessment. Your answers stay in your browser.

1. Do you have your own office, a dealer or an exclusive representative in the buyer's country?
2. Does your website show exactly the product or model asked for?
3. Can you prove deliveries of this size with references?
4. Can you provide installation, service or warranty in that country – yourself or with a partner?
5. Are there at least four weeks left before the deadline?
6. Can you supply documents in English and pay for a translation if required?
